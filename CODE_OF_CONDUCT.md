# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the HybridRoute community respectful, constructive, and welcoming for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, ability, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, clarity, and professional respect;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes rather than individuals;
- provide actionable feedback and accept reasonable technical disagreement;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible security disclosure;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them constructively;
- help maintain a safe environment for contributors, users, and maintainers.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- insulting, demeaning, sexualized, or deliberately inflammatory language;
- publishing private information without permission;
- sustained disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation;
- pressuring others to disclose credentials, private data, or vulnerability details publicly;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Technical discussions

HybridRoute includes security, policy, and high-impact routing concerns. Technical debate is welcome, but claims should be supported with reproducible evidence where practical. Disagreement about design choices must remain respectful and should clearly distinguish facts, measurements, assumptions, and preferences.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, security coordination, and other project-related communication. It also applies when an individual publicly represents the project or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using GitHub's private contact mechanisms when available. Do not include sensitive personal or security information in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove or edit content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, impact, prior behavior, and whether the participant takes responsibility and corrects the issue.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and common open-source community standards.
